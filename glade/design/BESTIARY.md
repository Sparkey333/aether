# GLADE — Bestiary & Rarity Codex

> Keeping the spirit of the Chao — a creature that becomes a visible record of your love —
> and expanding it into a whole living taxonomy: **Natures, rarities, chromatics, and a
> legendary pantheon.**

The Chao Garden's genius was that your creature *wore its upbringing on its body*, and that
the rarest form (the Chaos Chao) was a monument to consistency of love. GLADE keeps both and
goes deeper: every Bloomling sits somewhere in a design space defined by **two axes**, wears a
**rarity** earned through care, may carry a **chromatic** mutation, and — at the very top — can
become one of the **Named legendaries**. None of it is gacha; all of it is grown.

---

## 1 · The Bloomling, defined

A small, soft, teardrop-bodied garden spirit — part plant, part spirit. Big gentle eyes, tiny
stubby limbs, and a single glowing orb, the **Mote**, floating above its head that reads its
mood and nature at a glance. Everything below is a way the base Bloomling can grow.

## 2 · The two axes → the Twelve Natures

**Alignment** (from *when* and *what* you feed it — tied to the day/night cycle):

- ☀ **Solar** — warm golds/ambers, radiant, bold.
- ☾ **Lunar** — cool blues/violets, ethereal, dreaming.
- 🌿 **Verdant** — greens/earths, grounded, gentle (the neutral centre).

**Aspect** (from its dominant stat — how it moves through the world):

| Stat | Aspect | Body language | Signature morphology |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grace | **Tide** | flowing, water-loving | fins, smooth dewy skin, frills |
| Vigor | **Root** | sturdy, earthbound | mossy hide, little horns/hooves, thick tail |
| Wit | **Gale** | light, airborne | wings (moth/feather), wisps, tufted ears |
| Heart | **Hearth** | warm, close, glowing | soft inner glow, petal-crown, round cheeks |

**3 alignments × 4 aspects = 12 Natures** — *Solar Gale*, *Lunar Tide*, *Verdant Root*, and so
on. A perfectly balanced Verdant with no dominant stat stays a **Wildling** (the classic base).
Nature is legible but never forced: it *drifts* into place over a life of care.

## 3 · Rarity — how special an individual is

Rarity is **earned, not rolled.** It rises with alignment purity, total stats, morphology
richness, the rarity of essences you fed, how many **Returnings** it survived, and whether you
hit any secret triggers. Five tiers plus the mythic capstone:

1. **Common — *Sprout.*** Young or mixed; soft and plain, few traits, a plain round Mote. Every
   Bloomling starts here, and a well-loved Sprout is still the heart of the game.
2. **Uncommon — *Attuned.*** One clear Nature; clean single-type morphology; a colour-tinted Mote.
3. **Rare — *Radiant.*** High stats + strong alignment; a signature trait, a faint aura, and a
   **glyph-Mote** (the orb takes a small shape — heart, leaf, crescent).
4. **Epic — *Prismatic Heirloom.*** Cross-generational (survived ≥2 Returnings); layered
   morphology, a rare-essence mutation, iridescent edges, a **ringed Mote.** These are lineage
   pieces — a creature you can read three generations of care off of.
5. **Legendary — *the Named & the Everbloom.*** The immortal apex and the pantheon (§6). Gated by
   hard, discoverable, devotion-over-time paths. Target rarity < 1% of players.
6. **Mythic — *the One-Born.*** Eclipse / season / event uniques; at most one per garden, ever.

*Rarity is shown diegetically* — a soft frame of light around the Bloomling in the Almanac, not
a number. You *feel* that this one is special.

## 4 · Chromatics — the "shiny" system

A rare **palette-mutation** of any Nature: cosmetic-only prestige, triggered by a rare essence
combination plus a low seed-roll (surfaced only as a shimmer when it happens). Four named lines,
each stackable on any Nature/rarity:

- **Albloom** — luminous white & gold; looks lit from within.
- **Umbral** — inverted: black petals, deep-violet glow, star-cold.
- **Glass** — translucent, iridescent, a soap-bubble prism sheen.
- **Ashen** — soft ash-grey with ember-orange glow lines, like banked coals.

A Chromatic is the thing kids traded rumours about — *"I got an Umbral Lunar Tide"* — and it
never affects power, only pride.

## 5 · Motefall — variants of the orb itself

The Mote is the emotional interface, so its rare forms are prized: **Prism** (Everbloom),
**Ringed** (Epic+), **Twin** (mythic twins), **Ember/Frost/Bloom** (a tiny flower instead of an
orb). A Bloomling's Mote is its "eyes" — an unusual one is unforgettable.

## 6 · The Legendary Pantheon

Seven apex forms. Each keeps the Chaos-Chao rule — **you cannot grind it in a weekend; you have
to keep showing up** — and each is a small myth with a discoverable, deterministic path.

### ✦ The Everbloom — *the immortal apex*
Serene, ageless, wreathed in living crystalline flowers; a slow-shifting **prism Mote** that
cycles gold→blue→green forever. The Chaos Chao's true heir.
**Path:** hold Verdant balance at every cocoon across **≥3 Returnings**, every stat deep, never
neglected. It stops aging.

### ☀ Solaris, the Noonflame — *Solar legendary*
A regal sun-creature with a lion-like mane of golden petals and feathers, solar-flare wings, and
a small blazing sun for a Mote.
**Path:** a pure-Solar line across ≥3 Returnings, fed only sun-essences at high noon.

### ☾ Selene, the Nightbloom — *Lunar legendary*
Ethereal and translucent, great luminous moth-wings dusted with stars, a **crescent Mote**
trailing stardust.
**Path:** a pure-Lunar line, deep-night devotion, and one feeding during an eclipse.

### 🌿 The Greenwarden — *Verdant legendary*
A mossy garden-guardian with great antlers wreathed in blossom and vine, tiny birds nesting in
it, and a **living-flower Mote.** It tends the glade back.
**Path:** raise it while bringing the whole garden to full Verdancy and coaxing every bramble
into an arbor.

### ◐ The Eclipse Duskling — *dual Solar+Lunar legendary*
Body split warm-gold and cool-silver; one sun-wing, one moth-wing; both fins *and* wings; a
**ringed eclipse Mote** (a dark disc rimmed in gold light).
**Path:** perfectly balanced solar/lunar essences fed within minutes of the dawn/dusk threshold,
again and again.

### 🌱 The Dreamer (Old Seed) — *ancestral legendary*
A vast, sleeping seed-elder wrapped in vine, dreaming the garden into being — the "grandparent"
of a long, unbroken Verdant line.
**Path:** a single soul carried across *many* Returnings that never once left Verdant balance.

### ✦✦ Geminae, the Twin-Born — *mythic, one per garden*
Two Bloomlings sharing one soul, joined by **twin Motes** — born only when a Returning coincides
with a rare double-bloom night.
**Path:** unrepeatable; the garden remembers it forever.

## 7 · How this plugs into the game

- **Attunement + rarity** already exist in code as `alignment`, `stats`, `traits`, `soul`,
  `everbloom`, and the Returning — this codex is the *design layer* on top of those numbers.
- **Aspects** map 1:1 to the four stats (Grace→Tide, Vigor→Root, Wit→Gale, Heart→Hearth) and just
  pick which morphology family a trait comes from.
- **Chromatics** are a low-probability seed roll gated behind rare essence combos — a cosmetic
  layer over the existing procedural renderer.
- **The pantheon** are authored capstones the procedural system *aims toward*: when a hidden path
  completes, the Bloomling snaps to the Named design instead of a generic adult morph.
- **The Almanac** (a later feature) is where discovered Natures, Chromatics, and Legendaries are
  recorded — the collection meta that turns care into a lifelong hunt.
