The Design

The Honest Read

Read this and mean it. Serious Civil is a teaching and sketch bench — first-order Euler–Bernoulli statics on a rectangular section. It does not account for buckling, shear stress, torsion, fatigue, connections, lateral stability, dynamic loads, or your local building code. It will never be a substitute for a licensed Professional Engineer who stamps the drawing and owns the result. Use it to build intuition and check yourself — not to pour anything someone stands under.
