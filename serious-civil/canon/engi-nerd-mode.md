# Engi-Nerd Mode

Every power-up arc you ever loved was secretly about the same thing: the moment
the discipline you'd been avoiding stops being optional. Super Saiyan isn't anger
— it's a body finally doing what it always could once it stopped lying to itself.
Engi-Nerd Mode takes that metaphor at its word and bolts it to a load path.

## The rule that makes it honest

**Rigor is the only thing that raises your power level.** Not how loud you argue.
Not how confident you sound. Not how cool the section looks. The meter in the app
(`assets/engine.js → powerLevel`) only climbs for things that are actually true:

- **It stands up.** Peak bending stress under the allowable. (+power)
- **It's efficient, not reckless.** Utilization in the honest band (~60–85%) —
  not wasting a ton of steel, not flirting with the edge. (+power)
- **It doesn't bounce.** Passes the serviceability deflection sniff test
  (≈ L/240). Strength isn't stiffness. (+power)
- **Overstress tanks you.** Blow past allowable and the meter *drops*. Volume
  can't buy it back.

## Over 9000, and the tier above it

`9000+` is the ceiling for a clean, efficient, serviceable design — the literal
*it's-over-9000* payoff, earned by math, not by yelling.

But there is a tier the app ranks *above even that*: **TRUTH OVER GLORY** (`9001`,
reserved). You reach it by **refusing to fake a number you haven't earned** —
asking the engine for reinforced-concrete bending stress and getting a refusal
instead of a lie. Choosing to be right over looking right is ranked higher than
any perfect score, because it is the rarest discipline and the one the whole
trade is built on.

> The boundary is the playground. Push it, test it, redline it on paper all day —
> that's how you learn where it actually is. Just never pour past it because you
> got tired of the truth. White light, in truth and love: the truth part is the
> load-bearing word.
