# The Aether Fight Club — Safe-Parody Tournament & Training Ladder

> **The first rule of Aether Fight Club: everyone goes home fine.**
> The second rule: *you absolutely do talk about Aether Fight Club* — it's free YouTube content.

This is the build target the whole Codex points at: a **safe, tongue-in-cheek, frustration-release** competition that mines the highest-ranked **fun + sparrable + spectacular** styles ([Fight-Club leaderboard](13-grading-and-rankings.md)), with a training ladder from couch → VR → real ring. It is *Fight Club* re-imagined as the opposite of Fight Club: consensual, padded, refereed, and joyful.

---

## Design north star

Optimize the [**Fun-&-Safe index**](13-grading-and-rankings.md), not damage. The flagship in-house style is [**Flow-Strike**](14-fusion-lab.md) (Capoeira + TKD + tricking), backed by **BJJ/Wrestle-Jitsu** (submission grappling — our #2 fun pick, near-zero strike trauma) and **Blade Concord** (padded weapon sparring, fencing-safe). Banned by philosophy: anything that scores high on `injuryRiskTraining` and low on `safetyForSparring` (Lethwei, bare-knuckle, MGS-style neck-cranks).

## The Rulesets (three lanes)

### Lane A — "FLOW" (striking, point-control)
- **Gear:** headgear, 16oz gloves, shin guards, mouthguard, cup, chest protector. **Soft-contact to the head, controlled contact to the body/legs.**
- **Scoring:** points for *control, technique, creativity, evasion, and acrobatics* — **not** for knockdowns. A clean spinning technique that's pulled before impact scores **more** than a heavy hit. Knockout attempts = **point deduction**.
- **Rounds:** 3 × 2 min, generous standing counts, mandatory 8-count on any stumble.

### Lane B — "GRAPPLE" (submission, the safest lane)
- **Gear:** rashguard + shorts (no-gi) or gi. No strikes.
- **Scoring:** ADCC-style positional points + submissions. **Tap is celebrated, not shameful** — tap early, tap often, re-rack.
- **Rounds:** 1 × 6 min. Heel-hooks/neck-cranks **banned** at amateur tiers (the #1 grappling injury sources).

### Lane C — "BLADE" (padded weapons, the spectacle lane)
- **Gear:** HEMA/fencing mask, gorget, padded jacket, foam/nylon trainers (longsword, stick, cane).
- **Scoring:** fencing-style touches + control; **afterblow penalties** to discourage reckless trades.
- **Rounds:** first-to-7 touches.

### Universal safety rules
- **Three Safety Officials per bout:** a Referee (in-ring), a **Doctor/medic** (mandatory, ringside), and a **"Goose"** (a second who can throw in the towel for *either* fighter — borrowed from boxing, weaponized for safety).
- **The Tap-Out is sacred** and works in *all* lanes — touch your opponent, the mat, or say "Aether" and everything stops instantly.
- **Classes:** weight (±5 kg bands) **×** skill belt **×** age. No cross-tier mismatches, ever.
- **Hard bans:** strikes to a downed head, small-joint manipulation, spinal locks, eye/throat/groin, slams to the head, headbutts, real blades.

## Belt / Rank progression (the "Aether Ladder")

A unified rank that travels across lanes, themed to the Codex's provenance tiers:

| Rank | Name | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Folklore (D)** | First class. Knows the safety word. |
| 2 | **Tradition (C)** | 20 hours; passes a safe-falling + tap-out test. |
| 3 | **Scholar (B)** | First supervised sparring; one lane fundamentals. |
| 4 | **Measured (A)** | Competes in ≥2 lanes; coaches a beginner. |
| 5 | **Loom-Keeper** | Designs/tests a Fusion-Lab blend in the ring; community-elected. |

*(The ranks deliberately mirror Aether's A/B/C/D provenance doctrine — you earn your way from folklore to measured.)*

## The Training Curriculum (teach the top of the boards)

A 12-week "Season 0" onboarding draws straight from the [rankings](13-grading-and-rankings.md):
1. **Weeks 1–3 — Falling & Flowing:** Judo/BJJ breakfalls (safety first) + Capoeira *ginga* (movement, fun).
2. **Weeks 4–6 — The Hands & Shins:** Boxing fundamentals + Muay Thai pad-work (technique, no hard sparring).
3. **Weeks 7–9 — The Ground:** BJJ/Wrestle-Jitsu positional sparring (the safest hard-sparring on Earth).
4. **Weeks 10–12 — Pick your Lane:** Flow / Grapple / Blade specialization + first in-house "smoker."

## The Ladder: couch → ring

```
(i)   FUN GAMES   →  (ii) COURSES   →  (iii) VR TRAINING  →  (iv) REAL BOUTS  →  (v) YOUTUBE
 party/mobile        structured 12-wk    motion-tracked        padded local        free public
 "rate-the-style"    real-art curriculum  drilling + spar-sim   "smokers" → opens   content engine
```

**(i) Fun games — the on-ramp.** A web/mobile game built on `martial-arts.seed.json`: draft a roster of styles, simulate matchups using the stat model, "rate-the-realism" of movie clips, and a tier-list maker. Zero physical risk; pure recruitment funnel.

**(ii) Courses.** The 12-week curriculum above, partnered with existing BJJ/Muay Thai/Capoeira gyms (don't reinvent gyms — *plug into* them).

**(iii) VR training.** Motion-tracked (Quest-class) drilling: a VR coach checks your *ginga*, your jab, your breakfall; "ghost" sparring against recorded opponents at chosen intensity. VR is where you **practice the Fusion-Lab styles that don't have gyms yet** (Flow-Strike, Blade Concord) safely. Haptics optional; the win is **reps without damage.**

**(iv) Real-life tournaments, new rules.** Start with in-house **"smokers"** (unscored, friendly), graduate to local **Aether Opens** under the three-lane ruleset. New-rules innovations: *creativity scoring*, the universal Tap-Out, the "Goose," and **cross-lane "Triathlete" belts**.

**(v) YouTube content pipeline.** The whole thing is filmed and free:
- **"Style Lab"** — break down a real art + its movie/anime/game incarnation (this Codex, on camera).
- **"Realism Rated"** — score John Wick / MGS CQC / Avatar bending against the stat model.
- **"Smoker Night"** — friendly bouts, mic'd-up, blooper-friendly, *everyone laughing*.
- **"Fusion Lab"** — try to actually build & spar a hybrid (Flow-Strike, Sambo-Thai) and see if the predicted stats hold.
- **"Provenance Court"** — myth-busting episodes ([is Kalaripayattu really the oldest? Did the Gracies meet Maeda?](16-provenance-and-corrections.md)).

## Risk, ethics & liability *(read this part)*

This is the **un-fun but mandatory** section, and it is non-negotiable:
- **Consent, waivers, and insurance** for every participant; **medical clearance** for any contact lane.
- **A licensed medic ringside** for all contact bouts — no exceptions.
- **Concussion protocol:** mandatory sit-out periods; any head-impact = automatic stand-down + evaluation. We *measure and label* head contact like Aether measures everything.
- **Minors:** non-contact / VR / Lane-B (no-strike grappling) only, with guardian consent.
- **No real weapons, ever** (Lane C is foam/nylon under mask + gorget).
- **The parody stays parody:** this is frustration-*release* and community, explicitly **anti-machismo**. The culture rewards tapping, laughing, and coaching — not hurting people. Anyone there to actually hurt someone is removed.
- **It is not self-defense training and makes no such promise** — it's a *sport and a show*. For self-defense, the Codex points you to the [real leaderboards](13-grading-and-rankings.md).

> *Fight Club was about men hurting each other in secret. The Aether Fight Club is the inverse: people of all kinds, in the open, on camera, going hard and going home fine — and turning a global obsession with fighting into something safe, smart, and free.*
