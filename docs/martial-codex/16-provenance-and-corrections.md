# Provenance & Corrections — The Myth-Busting Ledger

Aether's doctrine is **"keep every layer, label every layer."** A martial art's history is one of the most myth-encrusted topics on Earth — nationalism, lineage-marketing, and movie lore all distort it. This ledger records the **load-bearing claims** behind the Codex, the verdict after adversarial checking, and the **myths we refuse to launder**. Tiers: **(A)** primary/archaeological · **(B)** scholarly consensus · **(C)** traditional/oral · **(D)** folklore/marketing-myth.

## Myths we report but refuse to launder *(flagged (D))*

- **Bodhidharma created Shaolin kung fu / all Asian martial arts.** Origin myth; debunked by martial-arts scholarship. The Shaolin *athletic* tradition is real; the Bodhidharma genesis story is **(D)**.
- **Systema is an ancient Cossack / secret-Spetsnaz art.** Marketing. It's a late-20th-c. synthesis with **no competition record**; it sits at the **bottom of every leaderboard** in this Codex. **(D)**
- **Naruto's Gentle Fist *is* Baguazhang.** The *resemblance* (Eight-Trigrams naming, circular palm strikes) is real and well-argued (B), but no Kishimoto statement confirms intent — so "based on Bagua" is **fan attribution (C)**, not fact.
- **Tai chi / internal masters with "no-touch knockout" power.** Pseudoscience; repeatedly exposed (e.g., Xu Xiaodong's challenge matches). Tai Chi's **health** benefits are real (B); the invincibility claims are **(D)**.
- **"Mount Toba" in Baki is the BJJ/Gracie character.** No — Toba is a **Giant Baba pro-wrestling** homage. The Gracie/vale-tudo thread runs through "Dickson" (← Rickson Gracie). Minor lore correction, kept honest.
- **Most "ancient unbroken" national arts** (Angampora's "33,000 years," various "oldest" claims). Real arts, **inflated timelines** — tiered (C/D) where the evidence is folkloric.

## A note on method (and its limits)

Research used live web search + source-fetching. **Several sources (Wikipedia, IMDb, Anime News Network, the Bruce Lee Foundation) were intermittently blocked by the session's egress proxy (HTTP 403)**, so some facts were corroborated via search-result extracts rather than fetched in full. Where a claim rests only on a snippet of a primary source we could not open, the tier was **downgraded accordingly**. Each load-bearing claim above is corroborated across multiple independent sources; per-section `### Sources` lists give the trail. This is a **(B)-tier synthesis** — rigorous, sourced, and honest about its own uncertainty. That is the Aether way.
