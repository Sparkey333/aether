endgame live in [`../../content/canon/resonance.md`](../../content/canon/resonance.md);
the *workbench* lives here. Each rung is a complete DIY guide written for a smart but
**forgetful** human, with two parallel tracks and dangers marked **inline at the exact

## How they climb together

Each coil's measured frequency, tune, and spark length is **Tier-A data** the Loom can
hold — the first hard, *self-generated* measurements in a repo that otherwise ingests the
world's. A musical coil (L2/L3) is a sound source for **Pyramid Temples** cymatics (Chladni
plates: frequency in, geometry out). **Jarvis** gives the interrupter a brain — MIDI in,
safe ramp-up, over-current protection, and logging. And **S1** keeps us honest: coils run at
kHz, *not* the Earth's 7.83 Hz, so the Schumann tie is a Tier-C correspondence plus its own
quiet measured build — never a property smuggled into a coil.

## The honest endgame

L4 points at Tesla's dream — the whole Earth as one resonant circuit, free power from the
[aether](../../content/canon/00-aether.md) anywhere, for anyone. Aether keeps that dream on
the map **and labels it honestly:** resonant coupling transfers real power at *near field*
(your L0 bulb, Qi pads, WiTricity across a room — Tier A); efficient *global* free power is
**not demonstrated** (Tier D). To honor the vision, refuse to fool yourself about it.
