# HOLLOWMARK — Ashes of the Warden Crown
### Game Design Document (condensed)

**Elevator pitch:** Two years a lab rat. One city to burn down. A whole world
to save by accident. A gritty sci-fantasy action-platformer in the mold of
Jak 2 — open-world hub city, gunplay + hoverboard + vehicles, a dark
transformation, and a revenge story that grows into responsibility — with an
all-new world, story, and cast.

---

## Pillars

1. **One living city as the spine.** Karrow, a dense vertical canyon megacity,
   is the hub; missions radiate into themed exterior gauntlets.
2. **Three traversal verbs, always available:** on-foot platforming, the
   **Skiff** (trick hoverboard), and **Drifters** (stealable hover-vehicles).
3. **A modular gun with four feels.** The **Splitfang** swaps between four
   cores — crowd / precision / sustained / nuke. Switching is the combat
   language.
4. **The dark transformation as power *and* curse.** **Hollow form** is
   terrifying but costs control and trust; the story leans on it.
5. **Mission variety as pacing.** Races, escorts, stealth, turret runs,
   platforming temples, chases, arena waves, bosses.
6. **Tone: dark with a grin.** Mature revenge thread; a wisecracking sidekick
   keeps it from grimdark.

## World & lore

The vanished **Wardens** seeded the world with **Vyre**, a living energy, and
left ruins, dormant **Oracles**, and rift-gates. Vyre strains: **Verdant**
(heal), **Crimson** (strength), **Azure** (speed/time), **Gold** (ammo),
**Hollow** (corrupting — the strain in Rook's blood), **Halo** (the rare pure
counterweight). The **Rend** — a biomechanical horde — pour through a sky-tear
called the **Riftmaw**, drawn to Vyre. Every Rend carries a glowing core.
Civilization huddles in **Karrow** under **Magnate Corvayne**, who harvested
Hollow Vyre and experimented on prisoners to build anti-Rend soldiers.
Rook was one of them.

## Story (three acts)

- **Cold open:** Rook breaks out of the Hollowmark Program after two years of
  Hollow-Vyre injections; Pip falls in a vat and comes out a critter; first
  taste of Hollow form escaping the lab.
- **Act I — The List:** The Undertow trades missions for a shot at Corvayne.
  Learn the city, earn the Skiff, clash with Vex, meet the Spark.
- **Act II — The Bigger Fire:** The Riftmaw widens. Corvayne's Hollow-Vyre
  stockpile is the only thing that can power the Waypost to seal it. Bellows
  betrays you; Sable defects; you storm the Citadel and kill Corvayne —
  the revenge ending, three-quarters through. It feels hollow. On purpose.
- **Act III — The Loop:** The Greybeard reveals himself as the Pale Warden and
  throws the Riftmaw open. The Waypost is a time-gate; the Spark is **Rook as
  a child** — closing the loop means sending the boy back to be raised alone
  in the wastes. Unlock **Halo form**; face the Pale Warden at the Riftmaw in
  a finale demanding both forms. Final shot rhymes with the cold open.

## Core gameplay

### The Splitfang (modular gun)
- **Maw Core** — Vyre-buckshot crowd clearer.
- **Lance Core** — precise auto-targeting bolt.
- **Swarm Core** — rapid-fire shredder.
- **Sunder Core** — chargeable arc-plasma that chains; rare ammo.
Plus melee combo (launcher on hit 3), dash, and a parry that refunds Hollow
meter on a perfect read. Cores upgrade with **Scrap** at Wren's garage.

### Hollow form
Fill the meter via Hollow Vyre and kills, then erupt: faster, stronger,
damage-resistant. Powers bought at Oracles with **Warden Sparks**:
**Hollow Lash** (shockwave), **Riftstep** (blink strike), **Maw Titan**
(brief giant form), **Hollow Sight** (slow time, weak points).
**The cost:** a corruption sub-meter rises while transformed; max it and you
briefly lose control. NPCs and story react to how much you lean on it.

### Halo form (Act III)
Hover/short flight, time-slow bubble, shield, heal. Hollow is offense and
dread; Halo is control and trust. The final boss demands fluent swapping.

### Traversal
On foot (double-jump, dash, ledge, grind) · the Skiff (grinds, tricks,
mid-air gunplay, races) · Drifters (two-height city traffic hijacking) ·
Wastes Crawler (desert buggy with turret).

## World structure

**Karrow districts:** the Verge (slums), the Undermarket (arena), the
Stacks/Foundry (industrial vertical), the Drowned Quarter (flooded ruins),
the Spire/Citadel. **Exterior spokes:** the Hollow Wastes (open desert),
Wardens' Reach (floating no-gun platforming temple), the Riftmaw Approach
(endgame).

## Progression & economy

**Warden Sparks** (orbs → Oracle powers) · **Scrap** (gun mods) ·
**Rendcores** (elite drops, lore) · **Relic Glyphs** (hidden collectibles →
art, lore, cheats) · New Game+ and a no-mercy hard mode.

## Boss ladder

1. The Vat-Thing (breakout / Hollow tutorial)
2. Commander Vex (Skiff race-duel)
3. The Foundry Colossus (vertical war-mech)
4. Brood-Mother of the Wastes (Crawler + on-foot)
5. Bellows' Betrayal (arena gauntlet, Hollow form disabled)
6. Vex Reborn (cyber rival)
7. Magnate Corvayne war-rig (false finale, end of Act II)
8. The Pale Warden at the Riftmaw (Hollow + Halo mastery)

## Art / tone / audio

Sun-bleached dunes against neon-grime verticality; bone-white Warden ruins;
teal/violet Vyre bioluminescence on rust. Stylized-but-grounded, readable
silhouettes, deliberately retro (PS2-era budgets as an aesthetic). Dynamic
score: electronic + tribal percussion (city), dust-blues guitar (wastes),
distortion + choir (Hollow). Hollow form desaturates and distorts the world.

## Modern updates over 2003

Generous checkpoints + instant restart (purist toggle), difficulty options,
aim assist, traffic-density slider, photo mode, full accessibility, NG+,
and an unforgiving "Ironwatch" mode for the veterans.
