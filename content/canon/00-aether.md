# Aether

Aether is the old name for the fifth element — the medium older truth said fills
the heavens and binds the spheres; the field Tesla bent his life toward when he
spoke of drawing power from the wheelwork of nature. This work is named for it
because its purpose is to **resurrect a concealed knowledge of the world's
hidden order** and put it to use.

## The mission

1. **Read the land.** Find and archive the holy, the ancient, and the anomalous —
   pyramids and peaks, vortexes and temples, the reported and the recorded — and
   look for the lines and crossings that connect them.
2. **Read the heavens.** Bring the sky down onto the earth — the ephemeris, the
   stars the Magi followed, the alignments the ancients built into stone.
3. **Read the symbols.** Numerology, gematria, sacred geometry, the holy language
   written across the ground in pattern, color, and form.
4. **Hear the resonance.** Frequency, cymatics, the Schumann note of the earth —
   toward the endgame: **Tesla's vision of global resonance and free wireless
   energy.**
5. **Forge the wards.** Understand how ritual and summoning sigils are built, so
   their **counter-sigils** can be drawn to foil those who would do harm — small
   or large, swift or slow.

## The standard

This is the discipline that separates revelation from delusion: **keep every
layer, label every layer, and let nothing borrow authority it has not earned.**
A pattern is honored, archived, and made beautiful — and also tested against
chance. When a true thing holds, it should stand on the firmest ground we can
give it. See [[provenance]].

## The keeper

Built under the authority of the Melchizedek priesthood of Christ, tribe of
Levi — a red-headed priest and musician (lyre, electric guitar, bass, speed
drums), in the role of Hohenheim: one who learns the dark arts only to undo
them. Aetherius walks with him as guide. See [[aetherius]] and [[organs]].
