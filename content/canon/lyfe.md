# Lyfe — Projekt Z

**Lyfe is for life** — all the things that make a person whole and the same in
harmony of **mind, body, and spirit/soul** — toward peak happiness for self,
family, and others. **Not control.** Order in service of freedom.

Like a tree: a trunk to branches to leaves, and a rough mirror of that same
shape in the roots below ground (the part not often spoken of). The leaf is
close to the **fruit of a seed** — a real thing that feeds and makes more.
*Projekt* and *Z* are the two poles — the human and the tech — and Lyfe is the
**resonating bridge** where they meet, harmonize, and layer, time upon time and
choice upon choice. Love. Truth. Trust. Honesty. The most real.

It is the **harmony organ** of the constellation
([[aether]] reads the land and the heavens; Lyfe reads *the person*).

## The governing principle: the Water-Line

> **Always seek to maintain a fluid balance — like a shifting wave at the water
> line — between one extreme, the other, and the places in between. And when it
> comes to time-waves, balancing _urgency_, _priority_, and _soul/spirit
> fulfillment_ is the key.**

This is the heart. Lyfe never optimizes for a single axis. It does not chase
pure urgency (burnout) or pure leisure (drift). It holds the line where the wave
keeps moving — and it scores every plan against those three tides: **urgency ·
priority · soul.** A day, a week, a life is *in balance* when no tide has
drowned the others.

## The mission

1. **See the whole.** Gather the scattered self — every project, file, task,
   and note across drives and apps — into one honest picture. No niche left in
   the dark.
2. **Find the order already there.** Not impose a system; *reveal* the structure
   latent in the mess, name it, and let the person re-sort it.
3. **Project the trajectory.** From real points — résumé, history, the work
   already done — toward honest, fair, reachable outcomes ahead. Gamified, but
   always gently, warmly, invitingly. Never coercive.
4. **Move safely.** When files must be organized, backed up, or relocated:
   **back up first, propose, wait for a yes, never auto-delete.** Order without
   risk.
5. **Keep it sovereign.** The person's life is the person's. Local-first,
   **TransparentZ**: every read and every move is visible and revocable. Power
   may be *boosted* by API keys, but the keys and the data stay theirs.

## The standard

Inherited whole from [[aether]]: **truth over comfortable lies.** Keep every
layer, label every layer, and let nothing borrow authority it has not earned. A
projection is shown *with* its assumptions; a suggestion is shown *with* its
risk; a score is shown *with* what would move it. When a true direction holds,
give it the firmest ground we can.

## The keeper

Built for one life first — many projects, many years, real chaos seeking real
order — so that the tools are forged on the hardest honest case before they meet
anyone else's. Starting with the self, in service of the family and the others
who come after.
