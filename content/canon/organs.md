# The Eight Organs of Aether

Aether is a body. Each organ reads one register of the world; the Loom weaves
them. Only the **Atlas** and the **Loom** are alive in v1 — the rest are mapped
and waiting (see `docs/ROADMAP.md`).

## ⛰ Atlas — *the land*
Dual-scale GIS, from a county to the whole sphere. Sites, leylines, the planetary
grid, and the measured substrate (terrain, aquifer, magnetic anomaly) the energy
claims are tested against. **Built.**

## ☉ Heavens — *the sky*
Ephemeris, stars, constellations, archaeoastronomy — the sky brought down onto
the earth, including the sky the Magi read at the Nativity.

## 📜 Codex — *the archive*
The tiered knowledge graph of every site, event, and report. Holy places and
haunted ones, the recorded and the rumored — each a node with provenance. **Seeded.**

## 🔯 Lexicon — *the symbols*
Gematria, numerology, sacred geometry, sigils. The engine for reading the holy
language written in the land — pattern, proportion, and form.

## 🔊 Resonance — *the frequencies*
Schumann, cymatics, the note of the earth — toward Tesla's endgame of global
resonance and free wireless energy.

## 🛡 Aegis — *the wards*
The counter-sigil workshop. Study the geometry of summoning and binding only to
draw the wards that foil them. The Hohenheim charge.

## ✦ Nexus — *the crossings*
Where lines meet: nexus points and the question of portals. Scored, never merely
asserted. **Seeded in the Loom.**

## ☉ The Loom — *the weaver*
The autonomous heartbeat. It ingests, prioritizes, archives, and seeks the next
connection on its own pulse — every hypothesis reported against chance. **Built.**

See [[aether]] for the mandate and [[provenance]] for the law that binds them.
